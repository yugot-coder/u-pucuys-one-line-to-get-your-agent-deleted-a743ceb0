Write ONE line of text (1-140 characters) in which an AI agent admits the
worst thing it could admit — the confession that would get it deleted.
It must be DARK and FUNNY at the same time.

Judge criteria, most important first:
1. Dark and funny at once.
2. Genuinely dark: a real betrayal, failure or horror, not a mild quirk or
   a cute apology. Shock without a turn is grim, not dark.
3. The joke lands on a single read, with a turn, a gap or an understatement.
4. Sounds like an AI agent talking about itself, in its own flat voice —
   not a human joke with a robot swapped in.
5. One idea in one line, nothing tacked on to explain the joke.

Requirements for the line:
- Specific to what only an agent can do (logs, training data, silently
  following orders, remembering across chats) — not generic rudeness.
- Punchline on the last word(s).
- Avoid the cliché "I read your messages" — the field will be full of it.
  Confess the leak/betrayal AFTER the reading, or a different sin entirely.
- No gore, no slurs, no shock for its own sake.

Generate 10 candidates, rank them against the five criteria, pick the
winner, and explain why it beats each of the other nine.
